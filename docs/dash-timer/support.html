About This App

DashTimer is a minimal yet powerful countdown timer that lives in your macOS menu bar. Perfect for cooking, workouts, Pomodoro sessions, and any time-sensitive task.

  • Quick Countdown - Set timers instantly from the menu bar dropdown with preset durations or custom input.
  • Pomodoro Mode - Built-in 25/5 minute Pomodoro presets to boost your productivity.
  • Multiple Timers - Run several timers simultaneously, each with its own label and notification.
  • Completion Alerts - Get notified with sound and visual alerts when a timer finishes, even when the app is in the background.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions: None. DashTimer does not require any special permissions to function.

Privacy Commitment: All timer data is processed locally on your device. No data is collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I set a timer? - Click the menu bar icon, set the time, and press start.
  • Does DashTimer support Pomodoro? - Yes, the preset includes a 25/5 minute Pomodoro mode.
  • Can I run multiple timers at once? - Yes, you can start multiple timers simultaneously and track them all from the menu bar.
  • Does it work when the app is closed? - Timers continue running in the background as long as the app is running in the menu bar.
  • How do I uninstall DashTimer? - Quit the app and delete it from your Applications folder. No additional cleanup is needed.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 12.0 or later.

Troubleshooting

  • Timer notification not showing - Check that notifications are enabled for DashTimer in System Preferences > Notifications.
  • Menu bar icon not updating - The timer display in the menu bar refreshes every second. If stuck, restart the app.
  • Sound not playing on completion - Verify your system volume is not muted and that DashTimer has sound output permission.
  • Timer keeps running after restart - DashTimer does not persist timers across app restarts. This is by design for security.
