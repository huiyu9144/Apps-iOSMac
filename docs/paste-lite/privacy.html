PasteLite - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how PasteLite collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

1. Data Collection

  • Clipboard Content: The app records your copied content history, stored locally on your device.
  • Usage Data: The app may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the product.
  • System Permissions: The app requires clipboard access solely for core functionality.

2. Data Usage

  • Provide clipboard history functionality
  • Support quick search and paste
  • Technical support
  • Product improvement

3. Data Storage

All clipboard history and configuration data are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your personal data to cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services

PasteLite does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. You will be notified within the app of any changes.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support page.
