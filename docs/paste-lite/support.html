PasteLite Support

We are here to help you with any questions or issues regarding PasteLite.

About This App

PasteLite is a lightweight clipboard manager for macOS that keeps your copy history at your fingertips. Never lose copied content again with instant search and recall.

  • Clipboard History - Automatically saves everything you copy, including text, images, and files, with a searchable history log.
  • Quick Search & Paste - Use the global shortcut to bring up the history panel, search by keyword, and paste any item instantly.
  • Snippets Management - Save frequently used text snippets with custom names for rapid access.
  • Menu Bar Access - Access your full clipboard history directly from the macOS menu bar.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions:

  • Accessibility - Required to simulate paste operations so you can paste selected history items into any application automatically.

Privacy Commitment: All clipboard data is stored exclusively on your local device. No clipboard content is ever transmitted to any server. Your data stays completely private.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my clipboard history stored? - All clipboard data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database.
  • Can I clear my history? - Yes, you can clear the clipboard history from the settings panel or set an auto-clear timer.
  • How do I paste an old item? - Press the global shortcut (default: Cmd+Shift+V) to open the history panel, then click the item you want to paste.
  • Does PasteLite copy images? - Yes, it supports images, files, and rich text in addition to plain text.
  • How do I uninstall PasteLite? - Quit the app, remove it from your Applications folder, and revoke Accessibility permission in System Preferences if desired.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 12.0 or later. Verify Accessibility permission is granted in System Preferences.

Troubleshooting

  • Accessibility permission not working - Go to System Preferences > Privacy & Security > Accessibility, remove PasteLite, then add it again. Restart the app.
  • Clipboard history not saving - Check that PasteLite is running in the menu bar. If the app was force-quit, history up to that point is preserved.
  • Global shortcut not responding - Check for conflicts with other apps. You can change the shortcut in PasteLite preferences.
  • App not launching at login - Enable "Launch at Login" in PasteLite preferences, or add PasteLite to your Login Items in System Settings.
