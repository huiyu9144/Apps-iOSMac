JiaRemoteWin - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how JiaRemoteWin collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

1. Data Collection

  • Screen Stream: Received screen data from JiaRemoteMac is displayed in real-time and not stored.
  • Input Events: Keyboard and mouse inputs are sent to the connected Mac and are not logged.
  • Network Data: The app communicates over the local network only. No data is sent to external servers.
  • Usage Data: The app may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the product.

2. Data Usage

  • Displaying remote screen content
  • Sending remote control commands
  • Technical support
  • Product improvement

3. Data Storage

All data is processed in memory. No screen content or input data is stored to disk. No data is uploaded to cloud servers.

4. Network Security

All communications are confined to the local network only. The app does not connect to any external servers or third-party services.

5. Third-Party Services

JiaRemoteWin does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. You will be notified within the app of any changes.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support page.
