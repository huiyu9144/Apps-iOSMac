About the App

GuanZhuZui (管住嘴) is a self-discipline habit tracker app. You set a challenge duration, pledge some coins, and check in every day. If you complete all check-ins, your coins are fully refunded. If you miss a day, the coins are used — a small price for the lesson learned.

It's not about the coins. It's about staying true to your word.

How It Works

  1. Choose your challenge — Pick the number of days you want to stay committed.
  2. Stake your coins — Decide how many coins to put on the line. Min 1 coin.
  3. Check in daily — Tap the big button every day. It's that simple.
  4. Succeed or learn — Complete all days and get your coins back. Miss one and the coins are used to reinforce the lesson.

Coin System

Coins are a virtual currency within the App. They are purchased via Apple In-App Purchase at a rate of 1 coin = 1 CNY (or equivalent in your local currency). Coins have no real-world value outside the App and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for cash. They exist solely as a commitment mechanism to help you stay disciplined.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase coins?
Open the App and tap the "Top Up" button on the home screen or the amount selection screen. Choose an amount and confirm the purchase through Apple's payment sheet.
What happens if I miss a check-in?
Missing a check-in means the challenge is considered failed. The coins you staked will be used. This is by design — it's the mechanism that keeps you honest.
Can I get a refund for unused coins?
All coin purchases are final and non-refundable, except as required by applicable law. Please choose your top-up amount carefully.
Is my data backed up to the cloud?
No. All data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the App, all data is permanently lost. There is no cloud sync or backup feature.
Can I change the challenge duration after starting?
No. Once a challenge has started, the duration and coin amount are locked. This ensures the integrity of the challenge. You can start a new challenge after completing or failing the current one.
