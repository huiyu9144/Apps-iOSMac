Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GuanZhuZui (the "App") handles your information when you use it.

1. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All data created while using the App — including challenge records, check-in history, and coin balances — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's UserDefaults system. No data is ever uploaded to any server.

2. In-App Purchases

The App offers coin top-ups via Apple's StoreKit framework. All payment transactions are processed directly by Apple. The App itself does not have access to your payment information, including credit card numbers, Apple ID credentials, or billing details.

3. Remote Configuration

On launch, the App fetches a JSON configuration file from a remote URL (hosted on GitHub Gist). This request is strictly limited to downloading UI text content. No device identifiers, usage data, or personal information is included in this request.

4. No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking services. Your use of the App is entirely private.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the App will permanently erase all associated data, including challenge history and coin balances. This data cannot be recovered. We recommend treating your achievements as part of your personal growth journey — they exist only on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated within the App.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at huiyu9144@outlook.com.
