QuickOCR Support

We are here to help you with any questions or issues regarding QuickOCR.

About This App

QuickOCR is a powerful optical character recognition tool for macOS that extracts text from images and screen captures. It uses the system's built-in Vision framework for fast, on-device recognition.

  • Screen Text Capture - Select any area on your screen to instantly recognize and copy all visible text.
  • Image File Import - Drag and drop image files (PNG, JPEG, TIFF) for offline text extraction.
  • Multi-Language Support - Recognizes text in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, and many more languages.
  • One-Click Copy - Recognized text is automatically copied to your clipboard for immediate use.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions:

  • Screen Recording - Required to capture screen areas for text recognition. macOS requires explicit consent for screen capture.

Privacy Commitment: All OCR processing is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No images or recognized text are ever sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I use QuickOCR? - Click the menu bar icon, select an image or capture a screen area, and wait for the text recognition to complete.
  • What languages are supported? - QuickOCR supports multiple languages including English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more.
  • Can I recognize text from a PDF? - QuickOCR works with images. For PDFs, take a screenshot of the page or export it as an image first.
  • Is an internet connection required? - No, all recognition happens offline on your device. No internet connection needed.
  • How do I uninstall QuickOCR? - Quit the app and delete it from your Applications folder. Revoke Screen Recording permission if desired.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 12.0 or later. Check Screen Recording permission is granted.

Troubleshooting

  • Screen Recording permission not granted - Go to System Preferences > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, enable QuickOCR, and restart the app.
  • Text recognition is inaccurate - Ensure the image is clear and well-lit. Higher resolution images yield better results. Try the correct language setting.
  • App cannot open image files - QuickOCR supports PNG, JPEG, TIFF, and BMP formats. Convert unsupported formats before importing.
  • Recognized text has wrong encoding - Some special characters may not display correctly. Try copying the text and pasting into a plain text editor.
