QuickOCR - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how QuickOCR collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

1. Data Collection

  • Image Content: The app processes your selected images for OCR recognition.
  • Recognition Results: Recognized text is temporarily stored for copying.
  • Usage Data: The app may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the product.
  • System Permissions: The app requires screen recording and file system access.

2. Data Usage

  • Provide OCR text recognition
  • Save recognition history
  • Technical support
  • Product improvement

3. Data Storage

All image data and recognition results are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your images or results to cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services

QuickOCR uses the system's built-in OCR engine for text recognition, with no third-party cloud services involved.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. You will be notified within the app of any changes.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support page.
