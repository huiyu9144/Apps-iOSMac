Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: The app won't launch. What should I do?

A: Please check that your macOS version meets the requirements (macOS 12.0 or later). If the issue persists, please contact us.

Q: How do I uninstall the app?

A: Open the Applications folder and drag the app to the Trash.

Q: Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

A: No. All apps run locally and do not upload any data to cloud servers.

Q: Do you support English?

A: Yes, all apps support English interface.
