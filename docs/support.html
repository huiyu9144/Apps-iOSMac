Privacy & Data Policy

Privacy First

All Apps-iOSMac applications are designed with your privacy as a top priority. Here is our commitment to you:

  • Local Processing Only - All data processing happens entirely on your device. None of our apps upload, transmit, or share your data with any external servers.
  • No Data Collection - We do not collect any personal information, usage statistics, or analytics data. No tracking cookies, no telemetry, no user profiling.
  • No Internet Required - Most of our apps function fully offline. Only apps with network-related features (remote desktop, network monitoring) require local network access, and even then, data stays within your local network.
  • No Third-Party Services - Our apps do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection SDKs.
  • Transparent Permissions - Each app only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for its core functionality. We clearly explain why each permission is needed.

Permissions Overview

Some of our apps require specific macOS permissions to function. Here is what each permission is used for:

  • Screen Recording - Required by CleanShot (screenshots), QuickOCR (screen text recognition), and JiaRemoteMac (remote screen sharing). This permission is used solely for capturing screen content and is never transmitted externally.
  • Accessibility - Required by PasteLite (auto-paste) and JiaRemoteMac (remote control input simulation). Used only for the specific automation features you enable.
  • Files & Folders - Required by FindDup (scanning for duplicates) and PicShrink (accessing images). You control which folders are accessible.
  • Network - Required by NetMeter, NetBar (traffic monitoring), and JiaRemoteMac/JiaRemoteWin (remote connection). Network data is processed locally and never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions (General)

Q: The app won't launch. What should I do?

A: First, verify your macOS version meets the minimum requirement (macOS 12.0 or later, some apps require 14.0+). Try moving the app to the Applications folder if you haven't already. If a "damaged" or "unidentified developer" warning appears, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway." If the issue persists, restart your Mac and try again.

Q: How do I grant permissions to an app?

A: Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security. Find the relevant permission category (Screen Recording, Accessibility, Files & Folders, etc.) and ensure the app is checked. You may need to click the lock icon to make changes. Some permissions require restarting the app after being granted.

Q: How do I uninstall an app?

A: Open the Applications folder in Finder, locate the app, and drag it to the Trash. For a clean removal, you may also want to check ~/Library/Preferences/ for any leftover configuration files (e.g., files starting with the app's bundle identifier) and ~/Library/Application Support/ for any app data folders.

Q: Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

A: No. All Apps-iOSMac applications process data entirely on your local machine. None of our apps upload your data to any cloud servers, third-party services, or external APIs. Your data stays on your device at all times.

Q: How do I submit a bug report or feature request?

A: The best way is to submit an issue on our GitHub Issues page. Please include your macOS version, app version, and a detailed description of the issue or suggestion. You can also email us directly at 361512234@qq.com.

Q: Do I need to purchase a license?

A: All Apps-iOSMac applications are currently free to use. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or license fees. If this changes in the future, we will clearly communicate it to all users.

Q: The app asks for permissions I'm not comfortable with. Can I still use it?

A: Some features may work with limited functionality if permissions are denied. However, core features of certain apps (e.g., CleanShot without Screen Recording, JiaRemoteMac without Accessibility) will not function properly. We only request permissions that are essential for the app's primary purpose, and we never use them for any other purpose.

General Troubleshooting Tips

  • Restart the app - Many issues are resolved by quitting and relaunching the application.
  • Check permissions - If a feature isn't working, verify that the required permissions are enabled in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • Update macOS - Ensure your system is running a supported version. Some features require macOS 14.0 or later.
  • Reinstall - If problems persist, try deleting the app and downloading a fresh copy from the official source.
  • Check for conflicts - Some third-party security software may interfere with app functionality. Try temporarily disabling such software to isolate the issue.
