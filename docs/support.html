Frequently Asked Questions (General)

Q: The app won't launch. What should I do?

A: Please check that your macOS version meets the requirements (macOS 12.0 or later, some apps require 14.0+). If the issue persists, please contact us via email or GitHub Issues.

Q: How do I uninstall an app?

A: Open the Applications folder and drag the app to the Trash. You may also want to check ~/Library/Preferences/ for any leftover configuration files.

Q: Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

A: No. All apps run locally on your device. None of our apps upload your data to any cloud servers. Data is processed and stored entirely on your machine.

Q: How do I submit a bug report or feature request?

A: The best way is to submit an issue on our GitHub Issues page. You can also email us directly.
