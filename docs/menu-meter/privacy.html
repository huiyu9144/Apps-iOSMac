MenuMeter - Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how MenuMeter collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

1. Data Collection

  • System Information: The app reads system performance data (CPU, memory, disk, network, etc.).
  • Usage Data: The app may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the product.

2. Data Usage

  • Display real-time system status
  • Provide performance monitoring
  • Technical support
  • Product improvement

3. Data Storage

All system monitoring data is read in real-time and is not persistently stored. We do not upload your system data to cloud servers.

4. Third-Party Services

MenuMeter does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. You will be notified within the app of any changes.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the support page.
