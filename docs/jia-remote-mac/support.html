JiaRemoteMac Support

We are here to help you with any questions or issues regarding JiaRemoteMac.

About This App

JiaRemoteMac is the macOS host component of the JiaRemote system. It runs as a background service on your Mac, allowing remote connection and control from a Windows client over your local network.

  • Screen Sharing - Stream your Mac screen to a Windows client in real time with smooth, low-latency video encoding.
  • Remote Control - Full keyboard and mouse control from the remote client, including click, drag, scroll, and text input.
  • Clipboard Sync - Seamlessly share clipboard content between your Mac and the remote Windows client.
  • Auto-Discovery - Automatically announces itself on the local network so the Windows client can find it without manual configuration.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions:

  • Screen Recording - Required to capture and stream the Mac screen to the remote client.
  • Accessibility - Required to simulate keyboard and mouse input for remote control functionality.

Privacy Commitment: All communication occurs exclusively over your local network. No screen data, keystrokes, or any other information is ever transmitted over the internet or to any third-party server.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What permissions does JiaRemoteMac need? - It requires Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions for full remote control functionality.
  • Is it secure? - All communication is over the local network only. No data is sent over the internet.
  • How do I grant permissions? - Go to System Preferences > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording and Accessibility, and enable JiaRemoteMac.
  • Can I use it over the internet? - Currently, JiaRemoteMac is designed for local network use only. Remote access over the internet is not supported.
  • How do I uninstall JiaRemoteMac? - Quit the app, delete it from your Applications folder, and revoke Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions if desired.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 12.3 or later. Verify both Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions are granted.

Troubleshooting

  • Client cannot find my Mac - Ensure both devices are on the same local network. Check that your firewall is not blocking port 5900.
  • Screen is black on the client - Screen Recording permission may not be granted. Re-check in System Preferences and restart the app.
  • Mouse or keyboard not working remotely - Accessibility permission may be missing. Remove and re-add JiaRemoteMac in Accessibility settings.
  • Connection drops frequently - Check your Wi-Fi signal strength. For best performance, use a wired Ethernet connection.
