About This App

HueSnap is a precision color picker for macOS that lives in your menu bar. It lets you instantly capture any color on your screen and copy its value in multiple formats.

  • Screen Magnifier - Zoom in on any pixel with a magnifying glass for precise color selection.
  • Multiple Color Formats - Copy colors as HEX, RGB, HSL, NSColor, or SwiftUI Color code.
  • Color History - Automatically saves your recently picked colors for quick reuse.
  • Menu Bar Convenience - Access the color picker instantly from the menu bar without opening a separate window.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions: None. HueSnap does not require any special permissions to function.

Privacy Commitment: All color picking is performed locally on your device. No screen data or color information is ever transmitted to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I pick a color? - Click the menu bar icon and use the magnifier to pick any color from the screen.
  • What color formats are supported? - HEX, RGB, HSL, and SwiftUI Color are all supported.
  • Can I save favorite colors? - Yes, HueSnap includes a color palette where you can save and organize your favorite colors.
  • Does it work with multiple displays? - Yes, HueSnap works across all connected displays.
  • How do I uninstall HueSnap? - Quit the app and delete it from your Applications folder. No additional cleanup is needed.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 14.0 or later.

Troubleshooting

  • Magnifier not showing - Check that HueSnap has permission to capture the screen. Try restarting the app.
  • Color values seem inaccurate - The magnifier shows the exact pixel color. Slight variations may occur due to display calibration.
  • App not responding after picking - This is rare. Force quit and restart HueSnap from the Applications folder.
  • Copied color format is wrong - Change the default output format in HueSnap preferences before picking.
