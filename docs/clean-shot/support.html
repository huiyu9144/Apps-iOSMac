CleanShot Support

We are here to help you with any questions or issues regarding CleanShot.

About This App

CleanShot is a powerful screen capture tool designed for macOS. It offers a variety of capture modes and annotation features to help you create professional screenshots effortlessly.

  • Multiple Capture Modes - Capture fullscreen, selected region, specific window, or scrolling content with a single click.
  • Built-in Annotation - Add arrows, text, shapes, highlights, and blur effects to your screenshots before saving.
  • Quick Actions - Copy to clipboard, save to file, or open in editor immediately after capture.
  • Menu Bar Integration - Access all features conveniently from the macOS menu bar without cluttering your dock.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions:

  • Screen Recording - Required to capture screen content. macOS requires explicit user consent for any app that records the screen.

Privacy Commitment: All screenshots are processed entirely on your device. No image data is ever uploaded to any server. Your captures stay local and private.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I take a screenshot? - Click the menu bar icon and choose capture mode (fullscreen, region, window, or scrolling).
  • Where are my screenshots saved? - Screenshots are saved locally to your designated folder, which you can customize in settings.
  • Can I change the screenshot format? - Yes, you can choose between PNG, JPEG, TIFF, and other formats in the preferences panel.
  • Does CleanShot support keyboard shortcuts? - Yes, you can assign custom global hotkeys for each capture mode in settings.
  • How do I uninstall CleanShot? - Quit the app, then delete it from your Applications folder. No additional cleanup is needed.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 12.0 or later. Also check that Screen Recording permission is granted in System Preferences.

Troubleshooting

  • Screen Recording permission not granted - Go to System Preferences > Privacy & Security > Screen Recording, and enable CleanShot. Restart the app afterward.
  • Screenshot not saving - Check that the output folder exists and is writable. Try resetting the save location in preferences.
  • Menu bar icon missing - The app may have crashed. Restart CleanShot from your Applications folder. If the issue persists, reboot your Mac.
  • Scrolling capture not working - Ensure the content you are capturing is in a scrollable view. Some web pages may require a short delay before scrolling starts.
