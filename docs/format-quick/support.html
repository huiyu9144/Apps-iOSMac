About This App

FormatQuick is a fast and reliable text format conversion tool for macOS. It instantly transforms data between popular formats, making it an essential utility for developers and data workers.

  • Multi-Format Conversion - Convert between JSON, XML, YAML, CSV, and plain text with a single click.
  • Syntax Validation - Automatically validates your input and highlights syntax errors before conversion.
  • Pretty Print & Minify - Format messy data into readable structures, or minify it for production use.
  • Clipboard Integration - Paste data directly from clipboard and copy results back with one click.

Permissions & Privacy

Required Permissions: None. FormatQuick does not require any special permissions to function.

Privacy Commitment: All data conversion is performed entirely on your device. No text or data is ever sent to any server. Your content never leaves your Mac.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What formats are supported? - FormatQuick supports conversion between JSON, XML, YAML, CSV, and other common text formats.
  • Is my data private? - Yes, all conversion happens locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.
  • Can I convert large files? - Yes, but very large files may take longer. Performance depends on your Mac's hardware.
  • Does it support batch conversion? - Currently, FormatQuick handles one conversion at a time. Batch support may be added in a future update.
  • How do I uninstall FormatQuick? - Quit the app and delete it from your Applications folder. No additional cleanup is needed.
  • The app won't launch? - Ensure your Mac meets the minimum requirements: macOS 14.0 or later.

Troubleshooting

  • Conversion fails with syntax error - Check your input for missing brackets, quotes, or commas. Use a validator to find the exact issue.
  • YAML indentation issues - YAML is sensitive to indentation. Ensure consistent use of spaces (not tabs).
  • CSV encoding problems - Make sure your CSV file uses UTF-8 encoding. Other encodings may cause character corruption.
  • App freezes during conversion - This may happen with extremely large inputs. Try splitting the data into smaller chunks.
